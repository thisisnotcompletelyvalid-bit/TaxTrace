# ADR-003: Borrowing does not inflate a user's tax receipt

Status: accepted

A government may spend more than current revenue. Treating total expenditure as though it were all financed by the user's current taxes would make a user's receipt exceed their liability.

Decision: the primary receipt conserves user tax liability. Borrowing is a separate financing source. Expenditure composition may determine allocation weights, but deficit finance does not create additional user tax dollars.
